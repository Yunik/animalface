Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

My results seem inaccurate

The AI analysis classifies your type based on facial features and is provided for entertainment purposes. Results may differ from your expectations, which is perfectly normal. You can try again with a different photo for potentially different results. Lighting, angle, and facial expression can all affect the outcome, so we recommend using a front-facing photo with good lighting for the best results.

Are my photos stored anywhere?

No, your photos are never stored. Uploaded photos are processed entirely within your browser (device) and are never transmitted to or stored on external servers. After analysis is complete, image data is automatically deleted from browser memory. For more details, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
