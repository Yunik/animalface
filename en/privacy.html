Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2024

1. Information We Collect

Office Worker Type Test (the "Service") does not collect any personal information from users.

Photos uploaded for the service are processed only within the user's browser and are not transmitted to or stored on external servers.

2. Image Processing

  • Uploaded images are processed only within the user's device (browser).
  • Image analysis is performed locally using TensorFlow.js running in the browser.
  • Images are not transmitted to or stored on servers.
  • After analysis is complete, image data is automatically deleted from browser memory.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This Service may use the following technologies to improve user experience and analyze service usage:

  • Google Analytics: Website visit statistics collection (anonymized data)
  • Google AdSense: Personalized advertising (may be implemented in the future)

These services have their own privacy policies, and data is processed according to their respective policies.

4. Third-Party Disclosure

This Service does not share user personal information with third parties.

5. User Rights

Since this Service does not collect personal information, there is no need to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information.

However, if you have any questions about using the service, please contact us at the information below.

6. Children's Privacy

This Service does not collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If a child under 13 uses the service, parental consent is required.

7. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be modified due to changes in laws or service policies. We will notify you of any changes through the website.
