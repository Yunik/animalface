Tips for Best Results

✅ Good Photos

  • Face visible from the front
  • Taken in bright lighting
  • Face takes up most of the frame
  • Clear image quality

❌ Photos to Avoid

  • Face is covered
  • Too dark or blurry
  • Side view or back of head
  • Multiple people in photo
