What is Office Worker Type Test?

Office Worker Type Test is a service that uses AI face analysis technology to analyze your office worker personality type as fun animal characters.

You can discover what type of office worker you are with just one photo.

8 Office Worker Animal Types

🦅

The Clock-Out Eagle

Master of on-time departure! You focus intensely during work hours, work efficiently, and leave exactly on time like a true professional.

🦁

The Leader Lion

A natural-born leader! With confident attitude, you lead projects and earn trust from team members.

🐼

The Overtime Panda

Dark circles are your badge of honor! Working diligently, you often find yourself staying late as a reliable team member.

🐿️

The Snack Hunter Squirrel

Where there are snacks, there you are! Stocking up treats in your desk drawer, you recharge with snacks and love sharing.

🐨

The Break Room Koala

The relaxed healer! Rather than rushing, you handle things calmly and value recharging in the break room.

👀

The Office-Savvy Meerkat

Master of reading the room! You quickly sense the boss's mood and team atmosphere, acting appropriately for every situation.

🦴

The Team Dinner Hyena

The team mood maker! Never missing a team dinner, you're the social butterfly and networking champion of the office.

🕊️

The Career Migrant Bird

Ready to fly toward better opportunities! You're passionate about self-improvement and prioritize career growth.
